My pitch is more about the opportunity to explore AI. Having access to the technology means - now that you know what it is, let's try it, see if we like it and if it is useful.

What we win is knowledge. If a big contractor comes with a slick demo, you'd know exactly what questions to ask, where the pain points are. That alone is worth the investment in local AI (like the DGX Spark costing 5k).

Here is my pitch idea and its five layers:

**Layer 1 — Knowledge acquisition**

> "We're buying 5k of education that we can't get any other way. By the time any vendor walks in here we'll know exactly what questions to ask and what the answers should be."

That alone justifies 5k. A single day of bad vendor consulting costs more.

**Layer 2 — Use case validation**

> "We're not guessing what AI can do for us. We're finding out empirically, with our own data, in our own environment, before committing to anything larger."

No risk of buying a solution to a problem you don't have.

**Layer 3 — Optionality**

> "If it works, we have a head start. If it doesn't, we have the knowledge to evaluate alternatives intelligently. If vendors come knocking, we can assess them. We win in every scenario."

Boards love optionality. It's how they think about every investment.

**Layer 4 — The honest framing**

> "We know what it is, we know what it isn't, we know where it fails and why. We're not chasing hype."

In a room full of AI hype this lands like cold water on a hot day.

**Layer 5 — Competitive positioning**

> "Our CEO can answer AI questions from investors with genuine conviction because we actually built something."

That has intangible value that's hard to quantify but everyone in the room understands.

**The 5k almost becomes irrelevant.** By the time you've made the knowledge and optionality case nobody is thinking about the number anymore. It's not "should we spend 5k on AI" it's "why wouldn't we spend 5k to be informed about the most important technology shift of the decade."

The best pitches don't feel like pitches. They feel like obvious decisions that were always going to happen.

In the end what I am offering is: for 5k you get your own local AI model that you can load documents and ask questions. **That is it.**
